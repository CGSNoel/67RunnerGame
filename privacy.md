## 2. The information we process

The information processed depends on which features you use, your age/privacy
profile, your region, and your consent choices.

### Gameplay and local data

The game stores some information locally on your device, including:

- Local coin-wallet transactions and balance data
- Run rewards and synchronization status
- Game settings, tutorial preferences, high scores, and cosmetic settings
- A locally selected age/privacy category, where you provide one

Local data remains on the device until you delete it through the game’s data
deletion controls, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the game.

### Firebase Authentication and online services

When online services are enabled, 67Runner may use Firebase Authentication.
Initially this may create an anonymous Firebase user identifier. If you later
choose to link the account, Firebase may process the identifier associated with
your Google or Apple account so that your progress, wallet, and entitlements
can be restored across devices.

We do not intentionally copy your Google or Apple display name, profile image,
or email address into the game database unless a later feature specifically
requires it. We do not receive or store your account password.

### Wallet, rewards, and entitlements

In this Privacy Policy, “wallet,” “balance,” and “coins” refer only to
67Runner’s virtual in-game currency. They do not refer to a bank account,
payment account, cash, or other real-money balance.

For online wallet features, we process information such as:

- Firebase user identifier
- Wallet balance and wallet version
- Transaction identifiers and transaction types
- Run identifiers, run duration, reward amount, and economy version
- Rewarded-ad claim identifiers and verification status
- Purchased coin and cosmetic entitlements
- Server timestamps and transaction-validation results

The server does not accept the client-provided balance as authoritative. It
validates individual transactions and maintains the server wallet.

### Advertising

67Runner may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging
Platform (UMP) to display advertising, including optional rewarded ads.

Depending on your age/privacy profile, location, and consent choices, Google
may process advertising-related information such as device and advertising
identifiers, IP address, technical device information, ad impressions, ad
interactions, and consent information.

For rewarded ads, the game sends AdMob an opaque claim identifier. We do not
send your Firebase user identifier, name, or email address to AdMob for ad
targeting. AdMob server-side verification may send us information needed to
confirm that a rewarded ad was completed.

We do not knowingly use personalized advertising for children, under-age
users, or users whose privacy status is unknown. Age- and consent-based
restrictions may limit advertising and online features.

### Purchases

67Runner may offer optional virtual coins and cosmetic items through Apple’s
App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment. We do not
receive or store your payment-card details.

To validate purchases and grant the correct entitlement, we may process:

- Store transaction identifiers
- Product identifiers
- Purchase tokens or signed transaction data
- Platform, validation, refund, and revocation status
- The Firebase account associated with the entitlement

Purchases are also subject to the applicable Apple or Google store terms and
refund procedures.

### Website contact form

If you contact us through the website, we process the information you submit,
which may include your name, email address, topic, and message. Web3Forms
processes the form submission on our behalf, and hCaptcha helps protect the
form from abuse. The form is used for support, purchase-delivery issues,
privacy requests, and other inquiries.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and the services used to
operate the website may process technical request information according to
their own policies.

We do not use behavioral analytics for the initial release of 67Runner.

## 3. Why we process information

Depending on the feature and jurisdiction, our legal bases may include:

- **Performance of a contract:** providing online accounts, wallet
  synchronization, entitlements, and requested game services
- **Consent:** personalized advertising or other processing where consent is
  required
- **Legal obligations:** maintaining records required for tax, accounting,
  refund, or fraud-related purposes
- **Legitimate interests:** protecting the game economy, preventing fraud and
  abuse, securing services, troubleshooting, and responding to support
  requests, where those interests do not override your rights

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does
not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

## 4. Children and age-based features

67Runner is not marketed as a children’s game, but children may use it. The
game may use a neutral age/privacy screen and apply different restrictions to
online features and advertising based on the selected category, regional
requirements, and consent status.

We do not knowingly use personalized advertising for children or users of
unknown age. If a child uses an online feature that requires personal-data
processing, applicable parental-consent and child-privacy requirements may
apply. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child’s information may
contact us using the details above.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is necessary for the purpose for
which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by
law.

- Account, wallet, entitlement, and gameplay-service data is retained while
  needed to provide the service and is deleted or anonymized following a
  valid deletion request, subject to legal exceptions.
- Support requests are retained only as long as necessary to handle the
  request, protect our legal interests, and comply with applicable obligations.
- Security, fraud-prevention, and rewarded-ad verification records may be
  retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate abuse and
  protect the game economy.
- Purchase and accounting records are retained where required by German tax
  and commercial law. Depending on the classification of a record, applicable
  German rules currently provide retention periods of up to **10 years for
  books and certain tax records, 8 years for accounting vouchers and invoices,
  and 6 years for certain business correspondence or other tax-relevant
  documents**. The period normally runs from the end of the relevant calendar
  year. We do not apply the longest period automatically to every piece of
  game data.
- Consent records are retained for as long as necessary to demonstrate and
  manage the relevant consent decision.

The exact retention period depends on the purpose and legal classification of
the information. We will delete or anonymize information when the applicable
purpose and retention obligation end.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information
against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse. Server wallet writes
are restricted to trusted backend functions, and store and rewarded-ad
transactions are validated before currency or entitlements are granted.

No local file can be treated as a complete security boundary. The authoritative
wallet for online features is maintained on the server.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion
- Request restriction of processing
- Object to certain processing
- Withdraw consent
- Request data portability
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

The in-game data-deletion control is the primary way to delete your account
and game data. Other rights requests may be submitted through the [Contact &
Support]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page or by email.

For residents of Germany, the competent supervisory authority may be:

[Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit
Rheinland-Pfalz](https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de/)

## 9. Deletion requests

The game provides a data-deletion option in its Settings menu. This is the
primary way to delete your data. The option deletes local game data and
requests deletion of the associated online account, virtual-currency wallet,
transactions, claims, and entitlements.

If you cannot access the game or the in-game deletion process does not work,
you may contact us through the support form or the privacy contact address as
a fallback. We may need to verify a fallback request before acting on it.
Information that must be retained for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention,
refund, or legal-claim purposes may be retained for the applicable statutory
period and restricted from other use.

Deleting an account may be irreversible. Store purchase records held by Apple
or Google are governed by those providers and cannot be deleted by us from
their systems.
